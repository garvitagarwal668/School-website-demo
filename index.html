Our Story

Built by a handful of teachers who refused to compromise.

Jaipur Public School opened its gates in 1979 with three classrooms, forty students and one promise: that a child's education shouldn't be limited by the size of a city block. Nearly five decades on, that promise has grown into a CBSE-affiliated campus that educates over 2,600 students from Nursery to Class XII.

We still measure ourselves the old-fashioned way — by how our students think, not just what they score. That said, the scores have followed: our senior secondary results have stayed above 95% for eleven consecutive years.

1979

Founded on Tonk Road with three classrooms and a single banyan tree that still shades the quadrangle today.

1996

Recognised for Senior Secondary education; first Class XII batch graduates with a 91% pass rate.

2009

15-acre campus expansion adds the athletics ground, auditorium and science block.

2018

New sports complex opens — cricket nets, an eight-lane track, and Jaipur's first school-owned climbing wall.

2024

STEM & robotics labs renovated in partnership with alumni-funded grants.

Campus Life

Twelve acres of ground. Zero excuses not to play.

Our athletics ground is the largest of any school in the district — big enough to run football, cricket and athletics practice at the same time without anyone waiting their turn. It's where we believe a lot of the real learning happens: losing gracefully, leading a team, showing up on a rainy Tuesday for practice anyway.

Beyond sport, 25+ clubs and societies run through the year, from robotics to Rajasthani folk dance, giving every student at least one hour a day that belongs entirely to their own curiosity.

Athletics Ground

An eight-lane track plus full-size cricket and football fields, floodlit for evening practice.

Performing Arts

A 500-seat auditorium hosts our annual fest, debates, and termly music & theatre showcases.

Robotics & STEM Lab

Renovated in 2024, with dedicated benches for coding, electronics and our annual science fair.

Debate & MUN

Our Model UN chapter and debate society regularly place in inter-school circuits across Rajasthan.

Scholarships & Concessions

We set aside part of every incoming class for students who've earned their place through merit, sport or circumstance.

  • MeritUp to 50% tuition waiver for students scoring 95%+ in the qualifying entrance test or previous board exam.
  • SportsUp to 40% tuition waiver for state or national-level athletes, renewable each year on continued performance.
  • Sibling10% concession on tuition for a second child enrolled from the same family.
  • Need-basedCase-by-case support through the Founders' Trust Fund for demonstrated financial need.

Scholarship applications open each March and are reviewed by an independent panel that includes two alumni.
